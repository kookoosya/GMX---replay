How to play Agar.io in the browser

You eat pellets, grow, and avoid bigger cells. The whole loop fits a five-minute break between timeline scrolls.

Core mechanics

  • Mass = safety — bigger cells absorb smaller ones on contact.
  • Viruses (green spikes) — large cells that hit them split into many pieces (risky but sometimes useful).
  • Split (Space) — launch half your mass forward to catch a target; you are vulnerable while pieces merge.
  • Eject (W) — feed mass to allies or bait enemies into bad splits.

Beginner mistakes

  1. Chasing tiny gains near the map edge while a larger cell cuts you off.
  2. Splitting too often — each piece is easier to eat until it merges.
  3. Ignoring viruses when you are top 5 on the leaderboard (one touch can ruin a great run).
