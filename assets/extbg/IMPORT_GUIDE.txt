Extension wallpapers import guide

Photo wallpapers:
- put/generated as extv3_XX.webp
- thumbs go to extbg/thumbs/extv3_XX.webp

Crypto/stylized SVG:
- use lux_ext_<slug>.svg

Free reserved ids:
- ext_free_01.svg
- ext_free_02.svg

Use Backend/tools/wallpaper_batch_helper.py to keep ids and thumbs clean.
