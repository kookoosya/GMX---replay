Top 10 .io games to play in 2025

Browser-first picks you can launch from GMXReply Arcade — no install, no account on third-party sites.

.io games exploded because they load fast, run in any tab, and reward five-minute sessions between posts. If you live on crypto Twitter, that rhythm is familiar: ship a GM, check charts, queue a quick match, repeat. These ten titles still earn a spot in 2025.

The list

  1. Agar.io — the genre blueprint. Eat, grow, dodge splits. Perfect warm-up.
  2. Slither.io — snake mechanics with neon chaos. Easy to learn, hard to put down.
  3. Diep.io — tanks, upgrades, and bullet hell. Great for players who want build variety.
  4. Shell Shockers — FPS with eggs. Silly premise, surprisingly skill-based.
  5. Paper.io 2 — claim territory by drawing loops. Short rounds, high tension.
  6. Hole.io — swallow the city. Satisfying progression in under three minutes.
  7. EvoWorld.io — evolve through the food chain. Good for longer sessions.
  8. Skribbl.io — party drawing and guessing. Bring friends from your group chat.
  9. 1v1.LOL — build + shoot duels. Mechanical skill ceiling without a download.
  10. Bloxd.io — voxel sandbox minigames. Minecraft energy in the browser.
