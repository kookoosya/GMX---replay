How it works

Three steps from handle to copied replies — the same flow you use inside the app.

  1. 1

    Connect your X handle

    Open the app, enter your @handle, and connect. Usage, referrals, and Pro codes stay in the app.

  2. 2

    Choose GM or GN

    Pick Good Morning or Good Night, select a style, and generate one or several replies at once.

  3. 3

    Copy and paste on X

    Copy the lines you want from the app and paste them into your post or reply — manually, on your terms.
