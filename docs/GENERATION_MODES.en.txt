GMXReply — generation modes (product spec)

GM/GN = reply to someone else's post on X (not a diary line, not a sign-off monologue).

Banks: GM ordinary, GN ordinary, GM crypto, GN crypto.

Modes
  min (Fast)
    One short line, postable immediately, no filler.
  mid (Balanced)
    Slightly richer; may add one short extra beat; still reads as a normal X reply.
  max (Full)
    Warmer / fuller tone; never essay-length.

Tone families (ordinary vs crypto) must feel distinct. Emoji only when it fits. Avoid repeated WAGMI/moon clichés in crypto; avoid bland diary tone in ordinary.
